How this board thinks - every factor, and where it actually lives

The rank is CVS: VOR plus a bounded, fully decomposed factor adjustment - CVS = VOR + z_point_scale x weighted-z of the non-projection factors, then the Walter judgment layer capped at the configured percent. Every row explains itself below; no hidden composite exists, and the anchor law is unit-tested at every merge.

DECISION INPUT - quality and opportunity
VOR from raw projections x this league's exact scoring is the anchor. Opportunity (2025 touches, WOPR), team context (PROE, position-signed), surrounding talent (depth slot), coaching (first-year-caller penalty from the curated file), and schedule modulate it by weights in data/cvs_weights.json - tunable, visible, never silent.
DECISION INPUT, CAPPED - the Walter guide layer
Judgment calls (target, sleeper, do-not-draft, regression, recency) apply as percentage deltas against the position's reference magnitude (the within-position SD of the pure model score - even authority across the range, at most 0.1 positional SD of movement at the cap), total clamped to the configured cap; every applied delta shows its verbatim quote and line reference below. Deltas that cross a tier boundary are flagged by name. Evidence from the guide (figures, mechanisms, situations) displays beside the number. Prospectively unvalidated - no historical guides exist to backtest - so the cap and the kill-switch (config walter_enabled, plus the live WALTER LAYER toggle on this board) are the risk bounds, stated plainly.
DISPLAYED EVIDENCE - never inside the number
Tendencies were backtested as a probability input and REJECTED (p=0.99); coaching prose, market bands, and vacated context render beside the rank. Walter's own figures are a comparison column, never blended.
NOT WIRED, ON PURPOSE
Historical priors (age curves, capital hit rates) are null for every player - the weight redistributes and confidence reports it. K and DST stay off the CVS board: their projections are floors (the reject list still bans invented completions). Title odds remain a labelled proxy.
